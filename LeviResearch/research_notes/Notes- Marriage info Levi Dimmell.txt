WED August 5, 2019
What follows are some notes from miscellaneous records I have about Levi regarding his marriages.  I was very early on in my searching when I went to Nova Scotia so I did not take good notes regarding attribution of information found.

These first two entries are from work done by a researcher whom Todd engaged to do some discovery.  #3 is a note from my own records.

1)   Marriage info:
According to research conducted for Todd DeMelle by Allen B. Robertson Ph.D. of Halifax NS in 2000 microfilm records (Micro 11282-X) Churches: Chester: St. Stephen's Anglican:

Baptisms:
p.33  born 1 June 1831, baptized March 1833:  Peter Levi son of Levi and Mary Ann Dimmels, Chester, Farmer
Marriages:
p. 38: Leven Demel - Widower - St. Stephen's Parish, Chester & Mary Ann Wilneuf of same place: married town by Banns 18 April 1826 By James Shreve, Rector; Witnesses: John Connet, Joseph Lorelly; Signed by Levi (his X mark) Demiel, Mary Ann (her X mark) Wilneuf


2)   The Surname of this Family: The research conducted by Allen B. Robertson Dr. Of Halifax Nova Scotia 2000 Microfilm Record (Micro 11282-X ) Churches Chester St.Stephen Anglican. Marriage of Stephen Leven Demel 1784-1858 1 first to Elizabeth Refus 20 Feb. 1821 By Rev. Joseph Dimock Baptist Minister .Widower then .Recorded by James Shreve Rector S.S.A.C. Married Mary Ann Willineuf 1784-1863 Banns 18 April 1826 by James Shreve,Rector Witnesses John Connet, Joseph Lorelly Signed by Levi and Mary Ann Demiel each with their mark X Page 38
